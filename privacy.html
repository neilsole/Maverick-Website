Privacy Policy

Last updated September 17, 2026.

Maverick Men's Grooming Center ("we," "us") operates this website to share our services, prices, hours, and location, and to let you book an appointment. This page explains what happens to information when you use the site.

What this website collects directly

This website does not have its own contact form, newsletter signup, account system, or analytics tracker. Browsing the pages on maverickmgc.com does not send any personal information to us.

Booking an appointment

The Book section of this site embeds our scheduling system, provided by DaySmart Software, directly in the page (and links to it at book.daysmart.com). When you book through that tool, you are giving your name, phone number, email, and appointment details to DaySmart, who process it on our behalf to schedule and manage your visit. That exchange is covered by DaySmart's own privacy policy, not by us:

  • DaySmart Software's Privacy Policy

Map

The Visit section embeds a Google Map so you can find the shop. Loading that map may let Google set cookies or collect data under Google's own policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. The embedded map and booking tools described above may set cookies of their own when you interact with them; those are controlled by Google and DaySmart respectively.

Children

We offer haircuts for kids in the shop, but this website itself is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Changes to this policy

If how this site handles information changes, we will update this page and the date above.
