About

A real barbershop, run the right way.

Located at The Block in Natick Center, Maverick Men's Grooming Center is exactly what a local barbershop should be. We offer sharp, classic cuts in an efficient, welcoming environment, so you can look your best and get on with your day.

Managing barber Daniel DaSilva cuts for all ages. Whether it is a kid's milestone first haircut or a long-time regular getting their usual, you know you are in good hands.

“Clean, classy and efficient.” Carl Cwiok, owner
